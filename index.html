Tracking coding time

We use Hackatime and Lapse to track your time to submit to Hack Club events for hours verification.

Setup Hackatime with my editor

Start logging your hours by connecting Hackatime with your editor. Hackatime can connect to any editor or application that supports Wakatime.

Start Hackatime setup

Setup Lapse time-lapse tracking

If Hackatime is not compatible with your editor, use Lapse to record a time-lapse of an activity to log hours.
